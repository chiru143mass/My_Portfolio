Complaint Guidelines

How to File an Effective Complaint:

  • Be Specific: Provide clear details about the issue
  • Include Evidence: Mention dates, times, and locations
  • Stay Professional: Use respectful language
  • Choose Right Category: This helps us route to the right team
  • Set Proper Urgency: Help us prioritize appropriately

Response Times:

Critical: Within 1 hour

High: Within 4 hours

Medium: Within 24 hours

Low: Within 3 days

Contact for Emergencies:

Security Emergency: Ext. 911

Maintenance Emergency: Ext. 123

Medical Emergency: Call 108
