Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in: 2:00 PM onwards | Check-out: 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request.

How do I report a maintenance issue?

You can report maintenance issues through the Complaints section, call maintenance directly, or visit the front desk.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, net banking, credit/debit cards, and cash payments. Online payments are preferred for convenience.

Is there a security deposit required?

Yes, a refundable security deposit is required at the time of check-in. The amount varies by room type.
